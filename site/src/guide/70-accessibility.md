You can use Warble with VoiceOver, and the keyboard's media keys control what's playing. It also follows the display settings in System Settings › Accessibility.

## Keys

The play/pause, next and previous keys on your keyboard control what's playing, as they do for any player. With the pointer on an open notch, <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes it.

## Use VoiceOver

- The closed notch reads "Now playing", then the song and the artist, and whether it's playing or paused.
- In the open notch, every button has a name. The position and the volume work like any other slider.

## Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion

- With **Increase contrast** on, grey text gets lighter, and the open notch has a solid border in the album's colour instead of a glow.
- With **Reduce motion** on, the notch changes shape at once and only its contents fade. The play-state bars stand still.

Increase contrast is in System Settings › Accessibility › Display, and Reduce motion is in Accessibility › Motion. Warble never uses colour alone to show something: shuffle and repeat show a dot under them when they're on.
