Warble works without changing anything, but a few things can be set your way.

## Open Settings

1. Click the Warble icon in the menu bar, or right-click the notch. You can also Control-click it.
2. Choose **Settings…**.

The right-click menu also opens the app that's playing, checks for updates and quits Warble. It works even with the menu bar icon hidden. To get the icon back, open Warble again from your Applications folder. The icon comes back, and Settings opens.

Every change takes effect straight away.

## General

- **Open at login.** Off at first. Turn it on, and Warble opens each time you log in, so it's there after a restart. It's the same switch as Warble's entry in System Settings › General › Login Items. If you turn Warble off there, turn it back on there.
- **Show icon in the menu bar.** Turn it off to keep the menu bar tidy. Right-click the notch for the same menu, or open Warble from Applications to get the icon back.
- **Text size.** Makes the words in the notch larger: **Standard**, **Large** or **Larger**.
- **Displays.** For each display without a notch, choose whether Warble draws one there. See [External displays](/docs/external-displays/).
- **Stay visible after pausing.** How long a paused song stays in the notch before Warble hides behind the camera again, from 5 seconds to 5 minutes.

## Notch

- **When nothing is playing, hover shows.** **Last Played** shows the card with the last song you played, so you can [pick up where you left off](/docs/pick-up-where-you-left-off/). **Nothing** keeps Warble out of sight until something plays.

> [!NOTE]
> Warble's animations follow **Reduce motion** in System Settings › Accessibility › Display.

## Shortcuts

The play/pause, next and previous keys on your keyboard already control what's playing. If your keyboard doesn't have them, or you'd rather use other keys, give each action a shortcut that works in every app. None is set until you add one.

1. Click **Record Shortcut** beside **Play or pause**, **Next track** or **Previous track**.
2. Press the keys you want, such as <kbd>⌃⌥P</kbd>. A shortcut needs <kbd>⌘</kbd>, <kbd>⌥</kbd> or <kbd>⌃</kbd>, so it doesn't catch your typing.

The shortcut works straight away. To change it, click it and press new keys. To remove it, click the cross beside it, or click it and press <kbd>Delete</kbd>. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to stop without changing anything.

Warble won't use a shortcut that every app needs, such as <kbd>⌘Q</kbd>, or one another action already has. It tells you why under the shortcut, so you can pick other keys.

> [!NOTE]
> If another app already uses the keys, Warble says so under the shortcut, or the keys don't record at all. Choose a different one, or change it in the other app.

## About

About shows which version of Warble you have, with **Check for Updates…** and a switch to check for updates automatically. Warble only goes online to check for updates.
