title: Pick up where you left off
summary: Play the last song again from the notch when nothing is playing.
group: Playback
---

When nothing is playing, Warble hides behind the notch, so your screen looks just as it did before you installed it. It still remembers the last song you played, even after you restart your Mac.

When a song starts, the notch slides out from behind the camera to show the album art and the bars, and when it stops, it slides back. If you've turned on **Reduce motion** in System Settings › Accessibility › Display, the notch changes size at once and only fades.

## Play the last song again

1. Hover over the notch, or click it. A short card drops down with the last song you played and the app that played it.
2. Click the play button on the right to carry on from where you stopped.

If the app is still open, the song carries on from where you paused it. If you've quit the app or restarted your Mac, Spotify opens and carries on, and any other app opens for you to press play there.

### Songs and videos in a browser

A browser can't carry on once you've closed the tab, so Warble can note the page instead. Open **Settings…**, click **More options** and turn on **Reopen browser tracks from the idle card**. Then the play button opens that page again in the same browser. It works with Safari, Chrome, Brave, Edge, Arc and Vivaldi.

macOS asks once for each browser whether Warble may control it. Warble only looks at the front tab when a track starts, and only keeps the page if its title names the track. Without the setting, or in another browser, the button opens the browser and you find the page yourself.

The card grows into the full player as the song starts, with all the controls.

> [!TIP]
> Click the album art on the card to open the app that played the song.

## Before you've played anything

If you haven't played anything since installing Warble, the card says **Nothing playing**. Start a song in any app, and it shows in the notch.

> [!NOTE]
> Only the notch itself opens the card, so moving the pointer across the menu bar doesn't open anything.

## Turn off the card

If you'd rather a hover did nothing while nothing is playing, open **Settings…** from the Warble icon in the menu bar, and set **When nothing is playing, hover shows** to **Nothing**.

When you pause, the song stays in the notch for 10 seconds before Warble hides. To change how long, set **Stay visible after pausing** in Settings › **General**.
