The notch answers a few gestures on a trackpad, so you can change what's playing without opening it. Each one only adds a quicker way to do something the player's buttons already do.

## Change the volume

Scroll up or down over the notch, with two fingers on a trackpad or with your mouse's wheel. Up is louder, and down is quieter. It works even with the notch closed, and it's on from the start.

## Swipe to skip

Swiping is off at first.

1. Right-click the notch, or click the Warble icon in the menu bar, and choose **Settings…**.
2. Choose **Notch**.
3. Turn on **Swipe over the notch to skip**.

Then point at the notch and swipe:

- **Left** with two fingers skips to the next song.
- **Right** goes back.

One swipe skips one song, however far it goes. On a Mac with a Force Touch trackpad, you feel a light tick as it skips. Swiping works whether the notch is closed or open, and does nothing while nothing is playing.

Left and right mean the way your fingers move, whichever scroll direction you've set in System Settings › Trackpad.

> [!NOTE]
> Swiping needs a trackpad, or a Magic Mouse, where you swipe with one finger. A mouse with a wheel can't swipe sideways, so use the skip buttons in the player instead.

> [!TIP]
> Warble decides which way you're going from the first moment of the gesture. Scroll mostly up or down and it changes the volume; swipe mostly sideways and it skips, without touching the volume.
