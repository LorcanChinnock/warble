title: More than one player
summary: Which app the notch follows when several are playing, and how to switch.

---

Warble shows one app at a time. When more than one app has something to play, such as Spotify and a video in your browser, the notch follows the app your Mac treats as playing now. That's the same one the play/pause key on your keyboard controls.

## Switch to another app

1. Press play in the app you want.
2. The notch moves to that app's song, and its controls now control that app.

> [!TIP]
> To see which app the notch is following, right-click the notch. The first item in the menu opens that app.

## Choosing an app yourself

You can't yet keep the notch on one app, or tell it to leave an app out, such as a video call. Both are still being built.
