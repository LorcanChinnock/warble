Most displays don't have a notch, so Warble can draw one of its own at the top centre of the screen. It works the same way as the real one: hover over it or click it to open it.

## What happens by default

- **On a MacBook with a notch,** Warble uses the notch while the lid is open. When you close the lid and use an external display, the notch moves to the display that has the menu bar.
- **On a Mac without a notch,** such as a Mac mini or an older MacBook Air, Warble draws one on the display that has the menu bar.

Other displays don't show a notch until you choose to show one.

## Choose where the notch shows

1. Click the Warble icon in the menu bar.
2. Choose **Notch on Displays**, then the display you want to change. The same choices are in **Settings…** › **General** › **Displays**.
3. Choose one of these:
   - **Always** shows a notch on that display all the time, as well as the one on your MacBook. When nothing is playing, it's a plain black notch.
   - **When There's No Notch** shows it only when your MacBook's lid is closed, or if your Mac doesn't have a notch.
   - **Off** never shows a notch on that display.

The change takes effect straight away. Warble remembers each display, so the setting comes back when you plug that display in again.

> [!NOTE]
> **Notch on Displays** and Settings list only displays without a notch. Your MacBook's own notch always shows what's playing.

## Full-screen apps

When an app is full screen, it has the whole display to itself, so the notch on that display hides until you leave full screen.
