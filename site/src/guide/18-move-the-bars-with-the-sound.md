While a song plays, the bars beside the notch move. Usually they loop, whatever the music does. Turn this on and they follow the sound instead: the first bar moves with the bass, the last with the treble. It's off at first.

## Turn it on

1. Right-click the notch, or click the Warble icon in the menu bar, and choose **Settings…**.
2. Choose **Notch**.
3. Turn on **Move the bars with the sound**.

The first time the bars show, macOS asks to let Warble hear your Mac's audio. Choose **Allow**. Warble uses it only to move the bars. It doesn't record or keep anything, and nothing leaves your Mac.

## When it listens

Warble listens only while the bars are on screen with something playing. When you pause, open the notch, go to a full-screen app or turn the setting off, it stops, and macOS stops showing Warble as listening. With nothing playing it never listens.

It follows whatever your Mac is playing, not only the player Warble shows, and it follows the output you've chosen, such as AirPods.

With **Reduce motion** on in System Settings › Accessibility › Display, the bars stand still, so Warble doesn't listen at all.

## If you said no

If you chose **Don't Allow**, the setting turns itself off and says so under it.

1. Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording**.
2. Allow Warble, under **System Audio Recording Only**.
3. Turn **Move the bars with the sound** on again in Warble's **Settings › Notch**.

## Troubleshooting

- **The bars loop as before.** Check that the setting is on, and that **Reduce motion** is off.
- **They stay low.** Something has to be playing out loud: a muted Mac, or a player that sends its sound somewhere else, gives them nothing to follow.
