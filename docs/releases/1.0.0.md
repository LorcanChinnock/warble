Warble 1.0 is the first stable release of Warble, a free app that shows what's playing in your Mac's notch and lets you control the music from there. Here is what came in during the beta.

### Highlights

- Settings is a standard Mac window with a life-size preview of the notch
- Warble has its own update window, which lists what changed since your version
- On a Mac or display without a notch, Warble draws one at the top of the screen
- When a paused song leaves the notch, pointing at the notch brings it back with a play button
- Clicking the speaker mutes the sound, and a heart favourites the song in Music
- Warble is translated into eight languages and works with VoiceOver, Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion
