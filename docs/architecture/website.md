# Website

Read this before changing `site/` or the user guide. Back to the [architecture index](../../CLAUDE.md#architecture).

`site/` is the Astro site for warble.lorcanchinnock.com, deployed by Cloudflare Workers Builds on every push to `main` (see `site/README.md` › Deploying), so it describes `main`, which can be ahead of the version people can install. The user guide lives in `site/src/guide/`, one Markdown file per page at `/docs/<slug>/`, in file name order. Each file's front matter gives its `title`, a one-line `summary` and its `group` (the groups are in `site/src/lib/guide-files.ts`). Write pages as tasks, in second person: numbered steps, `<kbd>⌘⇧4</kbd>` for keys, and `> [!TIP]` or `> [!NOTE]` callouts. The site also renders `CHANGELOG.md` directly. The landing page's `#install` section holds the steps to open Warble past Gatekeeper, and the Homebrew cask's caveat (`scripts/homebrew-cask.sh`) links to it, so keep that anchor. release-please ignores `site/`. The site's look is Warble's own, not Shot's: warm and a little playful, with a cream page, cocoa text, the icon's yellow and pink, a soft serif (Fraunces) for headings, Figtree for text and DM Mono for commands and keys. The notch is the one pure-black thing, as in the app. Light and dark follow the system, and dark is the same palette at night. The fonts come from `@fontsource` packages and are served from the site itself, because the Content-Security-Policy allows only `'self'` and the site makes no third-party requests. The tokens are at the top of `site/src/styles/global.css`. The landing page's notch (`NotchDemo.astro`, with `scripts/notch-demo.ts`) is a working copy on a drawing of a Mac's screen: it opens once by itself a moment after it scrolls into view, then waits for a hover, with no labels or controls around it, only ticks while it is on screen, and does not tour with Reduce motion. It plays nine real songs, the first ODESZA's "A Moment Apart", with their covers in `site/public/media/art-*.webp` and the list in `scripts/notch-demo.ts`; previous, next, shuffle, repeat, the position bar and the volume slider work on them. It draws the housing, wings and open card at the app's own proportions (`NotchLayout`), so change it when they change. It shows only the rest and open states, with no empty state and no captions of its own beyond one line, so keep them in line with the app's. Only the footer's "LC_" mark and "Made by Lorcan Chinnock", the voice and the copy button carry over from Shot.
