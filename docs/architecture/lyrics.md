# Lyrics

Read this before changing the lyric line, the lyrics card or the LRCLIB client. Back to the [architecture index](../../CLAUDE.md#architecture).

With `PreferenceKey.lyrics` on (it starts off), a resting notch shows the current lyric line in a strip below it, `NotchState.lyrics`, `NotchLayout.lyricsDrop` deep, below the toast in priority, so a track-change toast takes the strip while it shows. `LyricsState` (WarbleModel) in `AppState.lyrics` decides it: at the end of every `reduce` it takes the track shown (none while Idle) and asks for `.fetchLyrics` with a fetch number the first time a track plays, never for a pause, a seek or new art, and never for speech apps or a track with no length. `EffectRunner` asks the `LyricsProvider` port and parses the answer with `Lyrics(lrc:)` off the main actor, then dispatches `.lyricsLoaded`; an answer whose number isn't the latest is dropped, and turning lyrics off drops any on its way. While a line shows and the track plays, `LyricsState.deadline` is when the next line starts, worked out from the report and the rate, and joins the store's one timer, so there's none while paused, Idle or with no lyrics. `EffectRunner` drops a fetch whose turn comes after a newer one was asked for, so the latest is always asked. `LRCLIBClient` (WarbleCore) is the real provider: one request at a time, in order, and its cache in Caches is the only copy of any lyrics; they're never saved with the last track played. "No lyrics" expires after 30 days. Off, nothing is sent to LRCLIB. `LyricsLine` draws the line it's given. `LyricsState.excerpt` is the lines around the position (`LyricsExcerpt`: one before the line being sung, or the next to be, and three after, gaps left out), kept up to date with the line, and also while paused. While a song with lyrics shows (`AppState.hasLyricsCard`), the player's card has a lyrics button beside the output button, which dispatches `.showLyrics(display:, true)`. Then `AppState.Surface.showsLyrics` swaps the open card to `NotchCard.lyrics` until the notch closes, the way `showsDoctor` does. That card is the player's size and has the same button in the same place to swap back. `LyricsCard` draws the excerpt it's given. The `resting` preview uses `PreviewLyrics`, a hymn out of copyright, so it sends nothing.
