# Settings

Read this before adding or changing a setting, the Settings window or Open at login. Back to the [architecture index](../../CLAUDE.md#architecture).

Warble has six settings of its own. The first is Open at login: `LoginItem` (`Sources/Warble/Settings`) reads and sets `SMAppService.mainApp` and stores nothing, so the switch always matches System Settings › Login Items. The second is the title in a virtual notch, on at first: `NotchSettings` keeps it in `UserDefaults` (key `virtualTitle`) and dispatches `.virtualTitleChanged`, so `Notches.showsVirtualTitle` decides, in WarbleModel, which notch draws it (`AppState.titleInNotch`: a virtual notch, not over a full-screen app, while a track with a title shows). The third is how long a paused song stays, always at first: `NotchSettings` keeps `PauseDelay` in `UserDefaults` (key `pauseDelay`) and dispatches `.pauseDelayChanged`, which sets `IdleAfterPause.pauseDelay` (see [Idle](idle.md)). The fourth is when a virtual notch is there, only while playing at first: `NotchSettings` keeps `VirtualNotchVisibility` (WarbleModel) in `UserDefaults` (key `virtualNotch`) and dispatches `.virtualVisibilityChanged`, and `Notches.notchState` decides from it, for a virtual notch only (see [The notch](notch.md)). The fifth is how big the open card is: `NotchSettings` keeps `CardSize` (WarbleModel) in `UserDefaults` (key `cardSize`) and dispatches `.cardSizeChanged`, which sets `AppState.cardSize` (see [The notch](notch.md)). The sixth is the face of the notch's title, the system font at first: `NotchSettings` keeps `NotchFont` (WarbleModel) in `UserDefaults` (key `notchFont`) and dispatches `.fontChanged`, which sets `AppState.notchFont`; `Native.titleFont` makes the `NSFont`, and the artist and album stay in the system font. The title and the resting bars always take the artwork's colour, which is no setting: `PlayerModel.tint` is `ArtworkColours.text`, a quarter of the first artwork colour and the rest white, which keeps at least 7:1 against the black notch whatever the cover, so there is no lightening and no Increase Contrast step. `PlayerModel` always picks the colours, for that and for the glow, which is always on. Those six are the only settings the store holds. Everything else is fixed: the menu bar icon is always there and the notch's text is one size. `SettingsWindow` opens Settings from the menu bar menu, or when Warble is opened again from Finder. It's one page, `SettingsView`: the app's icon, serif name and version (plain secondary text, as on the website's hero) with Check for Updates…, then `NotchPreview`, which sit above the scrolling groups, a Notch group with Stay visible after pausing, Opened notch size, Notch on external displays and Song title font (each a `SettingsChoiceRow`, a picker with the title as its label), and the title switch, a General group with Open at login, the switch for checking for updates automatically (Sparkle's own setting, through `Updater`) and a link to the guide, then the acknowledgements. `NotchPreview` draws a virtual notch at the real notch's proportions from the real views, `RestingContent`, `ExpandedContent` and `NoteContent`, so it can't drift from the notch. They read a `PreviewNotch`, a `PlayerModel` and `NotchModel` of its own whose dispatch does nothing, and they are not hit-testable, so a control in the preview never reaches the real player. It shows what's playing (the shared `PlayerModel`, while `showsPlayback`) with its real title, artist, album and artwork, or `NotchDemo`'s made-up song with generated art when nothing is. It rests with the art and bars, shows the title when the switch is on, and opens into the card under the pointer, with `Glow`. A change to the title font or the card size also opens the card for `peekLength`, 2 seconds, so the change shows without pointing at the preview; it closes by itself unless the pointer is on it, and under Reduce Motion it doesn't open by itself. It stays as it is while the page scrolls (`onScrollPhaseChange`), so a pointer left over it doesn't open it mid-scroll. With the virtual notch Off it fades the notch out and leaves the bare screen. A change to the pause delay or the virtual notch's visibility pauses the song (flat bars, a play button on the card) and, after `PreviewEnding.beat`, ends it as `PreviewEnding` (WarbleModel) decides: the paused song stays with Always, otherwise an empty notch that opens the note with the virtual notch Always, or the bare screen. The beat is the same for 10 seconds and 1 minute, so the preview never suggests a shorter wait. That end holds until the pointer leaves the preview or the title switch, font or size changes, then the song plays again. The beat's and the peek's sleeps are the preview's only timers, apart from the scrubber's while the song plays; they run in `.task`s that end with the window. The preview sits on `Warm.wallpaper`, the website's yellow, pink and sky, so the black notch stands out; the notch itself is unchanged. The window follows the system's light or dark appearance and is the website's look (see [Design](design.md)): a cream page in light and the website's dark palette in dark, from `Warm` (`Sources/Warble/Design/Warm.swift`), whose `Adaptive` colours also step up with Increase Contrast. `SettingsWindow` sets `Warm.windowFill` as the window's own fill, so it is never white or black before SwiftUI draws. Its look is the controls in `Sources/Warble/Design/SettingsControls.swift`: `SettingsGroup` is a rounded card of rows told apart by hairlines that opens with a serif heading on an icon tile, one of the website's three accents (`tint`: Notch yellow, General sky, Acknowledgements pink), so every card reads the same way; `SettingsRow` is a title, an optional second line and a control; and `SettingsToggleRow`, `SettingsChoiceRow` and the `.warble` and `.warbleQuiet` buttons are the controls. Yellow marks what is on or chosen, along with position, weight and a thin ink edge (`Warm.accentEdge`), because on the cream page yellow alone is 1.2:1 against its track; the edge is invisible in dark, where yellow is 9:1. A pressed option answers too: the chosen one sinks into its lip, another darkens. The name, version and `NotchPreview` are pinned at the top (`SettingsView.pinned`), clear of the transparent title bar, and only the groups scroll beneath them, under a short fade, so a change shows without scrolling back up. The window resizes down to `SettingsWindow.minimumSize` and its page stops growing at 640 points. Add a setting as a row in a group, not as a standard `Toggle` or `Picker`. The window takes a Dock icon while open and drops its content when it closes.
