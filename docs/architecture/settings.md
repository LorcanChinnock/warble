# Settings

Read this before adding or changing a setting, the Settings window, shortcuts or Open at login. Back to the [architecture index](../../CLAUDE.md#architecture).

Keys and defaults live in WarbleCore `Preferences`. Views read them with `@Setting(PreferenceKey.x)`, which takes the default `Preferences` registers, from the `SettingsStore` (`Sources/Warble/Settings`) in the SwiftUI environment; `NSView(hosting:settings:)` hands it to every hosted view. A test checks every such key has a registered default. `SettingsWindow` opens Settings from the menu bar menu, or when Warble is opened again from Finder: a row of tabs (General, Shortcuts, About) above the chosen `SettingsSection`, always dark like the notch whatever the system appearance (the window is `darkAqua` on black). Its look is the controls in `Sources/Warble/Design/SettingsControls.swift`: `SettingsGroup` is a rounded panel of rows told apart by hairlines, `SettingsRow` a title, an optional second line and a control, and `SettingsToggleRow`, `SettingsMenuRow`, `WarbleSegmented` and `.warble` buttons are the controls. A row whose control speaks for it hides its text from VoiceOver and gives the title as the label and the second line as the hint. Add a setting as a row in a group, not as a standard `Toggle` or `Picker`. It takes a Dock icon while open and drops its content when it closes. Shortcuts has a row for each `WarbleAction` (`ShortcutRow`). `ShortcutRecorder` watches keys and clicks only while it records, and pauses `HotkeyCenter` meanwhile. `ShortcutRecording` (WarbleModel) decides what a pressed key does: cancel, clear, ask for a modifier, refuse a reserved or taken combo, or record it. `HotkeyCenter.start()` registers the shortcuts at launch and again whenever a `hotkey.*` key changes, exclusively, so macOS refuses one another app holds exclusively, and Settings shows it. Open at login is `LoginItem` (`Sources/Warble/Settings`), which reads and sets `SMAppService.mainApp` and stores nothing of its own, so it always matches System Settings › Login Items. General holds what a listener changes (Open at login, Text size, how long a paused song stays, the idle hover, the track-change message, podcast skips, the displays rules), and every extra starts off. A page or row arrives with its feature; Players comes later.
