# Settings

Read this before adding or changing a setting, the Settings window or Open at login. Back to the [architecture index](../../CLAUDE.md#architecture).

Warble has one setting of its own, Open at login. `LoginItem` (`Sources/Warble/Settings`) reads and sets `SMAppService.mainApp` and stores nothing, so the switch always matches System Settings › Login Items. Everything else is fixed: a paused song stays for `IdleAfterPause.delay` (10 seconds), the menu bar icon is always there and the notch's text is one size. `SettingsWindow` opens Settings from the menu bar menu, or when Warble is opened again from Finder. It's one page, `SettingsView`: the app's icon and version with Check for Updates…, then a group with Open at login, the switch for checking for updates automatically (Sparkle's own setting, through `Updater`) and a link to the guide, then the acknowledgements. The window is always dark like the notch, whatever the system appearance (it is `darkAqua` on black). Its look is the controls in `Sources/Warble/Design/SettingsControls.swift`: `SettingsGroup` is a rounded panel of rows told apart by hairlines, `SettingsRow` a title, an optional second line and a control, and `SettingsToggleRow` and `.warble` buttons are the controls. A row whose control speaks for it hides its text from VoiceOver and gives the title as the label and the second line as the hint. Add a setting as a row in a group, not as a standard `Toggle` or `Picker`. The window takes a Dock icon while open and drops its content when it closes.
