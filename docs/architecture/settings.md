# Settings

Read this before adding or changing a setting, the Settings window or Open at login. Back to the [architecture index](../../CLAUDE.md#architecture).

Warble has four settings of its own. The first is Open at login: `LoginItem` (`Sources/Warble/Settings`) reads and sets `SMAppService.mainApp` and stores nothing, so the switch always matches System Settings › Login Items. The second is the title in a virtual notch, off at first: `NotchSettings` keeps it in `UserDefaults` (key `virtualTitle`) and dispatches `.virtualTitleChanged`, so `Notches.showsVirtualTitle` decides, in WarbleModel, which notch draws it (`AppState.titleInNotch`: a virtual notch, not over a full-screen app, while a track with a title shows). The third is how long a paused song stays, 10 seconds at first: `NotchSettings` keeps `PauseDelay` in `UserDefaults` (key `pauseDelay`) and dispatches `.pauseDelayChanged`, which sets `IdleAfterPause.pauseDelay` (see [Idle](idle.md)). The fourth is the album-art glow, on at first: `NotchSettings` keeps it in `UserDefaults` (key `albumArtGlow`) and dispatches `.glowChanged`, which sets `AppState.showsGlow`. `PlayerModel` skips picking the artwork's colours while it is off, unless Increase Contrast is on, since the solid border it draws instead of the glow is made from them (it picks again when either changes), and `Glow` draws nothing but that border. Those three are the only settings the store holds. Everything else is fixed: the menu bar icon is always there and the notch's text is one size. `SettingsWindow` opens Settings from the menu bar menu, or when Warble is opened again from Finder. It's one page, `SettingsView`: the app's icon and version with Check for Updates…, then `NotchPreview`, a Notch group with Stay visible after pausing (`SettingsChoiceRow`, a picker with the title as its label), the title switch and the glow switch, a group with Open at login, the switch for checking for updates automatically (Sparkle's own setting, through `Updater`) and a link to the guide, then the acknowledgements. `NotchPreview` draws a virtual notch at the real notch's proportions with a made-up song (`NotchDemo`), never what's playing: it rests with the art and bars, shows the title when the switch is on, and opens into the card under the pointer, with `Glow` unless the glow switch is off. It sets no timers and nothing in it moves by itself, so it doesn't show the pause delay. The window's background is black with the same `Glow` at 70% from `NotchDemo`'s colours, left out with Increase Contrast or when the glow switch is off. The window is always dark like the notch, whatever the system appearance (it is `darkAqua` on black). Its look is the controls in `Sources/Warble/Design/SettingsControls.swift`: `SettingsGroup` is a rounded panel of rows told apart by hairlines, `SettingsRow` a title, an optional second line and a control, and `SettingsToggleRow` and `.warble` buttons are the controls. A row whose control speaks for it hides its text from VoiceOver and gives the title as the label and the second line as the hint. Add a setting as a row in a group, not as a standard `Toggle` or `Picker`. The window takes a Dock icon while open and drops its content when it closes.
