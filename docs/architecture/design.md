# Design

Read this before changing how anything looks, or adding a colour, font or control. Back to the [architecture index](../../CLAUDE.md#architecture).

Warble's look is Native, its own and not Shot's; don't bring over Shot's Brutal tokens or controls. The notch is pure black so it merges with the camera housing, with the system font, white marks and grey for anything secondary, and no accent colour. Its tokens live in `Native` (`Sources/Warble/Design/Native.swift`), beside `NSView(hosting:)`. Fonts are the system's at fixed sizes. Settings is not: it takes the website's look (`site/src/styles/global.css`) and follows the system's light or dark appearance, from `Warm` (`Sources/Warble/Design/Warm.swift`): a cream page and cocoa text, the icon's yellow for anything on or chosen (with a thin ink edge in light, `Warm.accentEdge`, so a chosen state has a 3:1 boundary), all three accents for the cards' icon tiles as on the website, the system serif for headings, and chunky pill buttons that stand on a lip and sink when pressed. The notch preview inside it is the one black thing, on a pastel wallpaper. Its controls are in `SettingsControls.swift`. Keep the two palettes apart: `Native` for the notch, `Warm` for Settings. Text on a yellow, pink or sky fill is always `Warm.onAccent`, in light and dark.

A control in the notch answers the pointer with `hoverWash` (`NotchButtonStyle.swift`): a faint white wash, 14% over the control's shape, that eases in over 0.12 s and snaps with Reduce Motion. Use it on any new tappable item in the card.
