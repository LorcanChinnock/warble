# Design

Read this before changing how anything looks, or adding a colour, font or control. Back to the [architecture index](../../CLAUDE.md#architecture).

Warble's look is Native, its own and not Shot's; don't bring over Shot's Brutal tokens or controls. The notch is pure black so it merges with the camera housing, with the system font, white marks and grey for anything secondary, and no accent colour. Its tokens live in `Native` (`Sources/Warble/Design/Native.swift`), beside `NSView(hosting:)` and `ScaledFont`, which follows Settings › General › Text size. It scales the notch's own words (the scrubber's times, the idle card's captions and hints, the Doctor card) and leaves content at its size (track titles and artists). Use `.font(.scaled(…))` for the first kind and a plain `Font` for the second. Settings is the same black and white, always dark, with its own controls (`SettingsControls.swift`): the chosen tab and buttons use the soft white `Native.selection`, groups of rows `Native.group`, and a switch is white when on.
