# Design

Read this before changing how anything looks, or adding a colour, font or control. Back to the [architecture index](../../CLAUDE.md#architecture).

Warble's look is Native, its own and not Shot's; don't bring over Shot's Brutal tokens or controls. The notch is pure black so it merges with the camera housing, with the system font, white marks and grey for anything secondary, and no accent colour. The glow in the card takes the artwork's colour (`ArtworkColours`) and, while a song plays, drifts (`DriftGlow`; see [Motion](motion.md)). The title and the resting play-state bars take it too, a quarter of the colour and the rest white, which stays above 7:1 against black whatever the cover. That colour is `PlayerModel.tint`, from `ArtworkColours.text`, fading to a new track's colour, and white until the colours are picked. Titles are set in the face Settings chose (`NotchFont`: the system font, rounded or monospaced) through `Native.titleFont`, while the artist, album and everything else stay in the system sans. Its tokens live in `Native` (`Sources/Warble/Design/Native.swift`), beside `NSView(hosting:)`. Fonts are the system's (sans, plus the serif for titles) at fixed sizes. Warble's windows are native: Settings uses system colours, fonts and controls (grouped forms, switches, pop-up menus, segmented controls, toolbar tabs), so it follows light and dark, Increase Contrast and Reduce Transparency by itself. The one colour of its own is the icon's yellow, `#F6C744`, the `Yellow` colour set in `Assets.xcassets`, as `Color.warbleYellow` (`Colour.swift`). It marks what's on or chosen: a switch that's on and the chosen segment, through `.tint`, set on each control by `accentSwitch()`, plus About's Update… button and the dot beside "Version … is ready". That dot has the spec's accent edge, 0.5 points of 28% black, in the light appearance only; the button's label is dark, as the spec's text on accent is. The status line's other dots are `systemGreen` for up to date and `systemGray` for couldn't check, no feed and last checked. It isn't the app's accent colour: the system draws the selected toolbar tab's label and links in the accent, and text is never yellow, so those keep the person's own accent from System Settings › Appearance. The notch preview is the one expressive thing in a window: the black notch on a pastel strip, the same in light and dark. The website keeps its warm cream look (`site/src/styles/global.css`); don't bring it into the app. Keep the two apart: `Native` for the notch, system colours and `Color.warbleYellow` for windows.

A control in the notch answers the pointer with `hoverWash` (`NotchButtonStyle.swift`): a faint white wash, 14% over the control's shape, that eases in over 0.12 s and snaps with Reduce Motion. Use it on any new tappable item in the card.

The playback bar's filled part ends in its own rounded tip, like the volume bar, with no knob. With the pointer over the bar, or while it's dragged, the tip grows into a 9 pt knob over `ScrubberHandle.growDuration` (at once with Reduce Motion), so there's a bigger handle to drag. The bar and knob are one outline (`BarFill` in `Scrubber.swift`, from `ScrubberHandle` in WarbleModel), joined by a curve on each side, because a circle drawn over a separate bar leaves a dark dip where its edge curves away from the bar's.
