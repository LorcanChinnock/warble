# Motion

Read this before changing how the notch moves between states. Back to the [architecture index](../../CLAUDE.md#architecture).

After `NotchSurface.apply(_:)` takes the store's state, `NotchSurface.update` sets `NotchModel.state` and `isPlaying` together inside one `Native.morph`, so every change of state, such as playback stopping under the open notch, morphs the shape and cross-fades the content; it grows the panel before the morph and shrinks it after, and `NotchPanel.show` lays the content out at the new size first so the shape stays centred. Content is keyed by `ContentID` and comes and goes with `ContentTransition`: in after `NotchMotion.contentDelay`, out in `contentOut`. What it shows is passed in as values, so content on its way out keeps the track that stopped. As the resting wings appear, the art grows from `NotchMotion.artScale` and `BarsView` raises the bars one after another (`NotchMotion.barDelay`) in Core Animation, then loops them at 8 fps; they drop as the notch leaves Resting, and their animations go with the view. The timings live in `NotchMotion` (WarbleModel). With Reduce Motion the shape changes at once and only the content fades.
