# Motion

Read this before changing how the notch moves between states. Back to the [architecture index](../../CLAUDE.md#architecture).

After `NotchSurface.apply(_:)` takes the store's state, `NotchSurface.update` sets `NotchModel.state` and `isPlaying` together inside one `Native.morph`, so every change of state, such as playback stopping under the open notch, morphs the shape and cross-fades the content; it grows the panel before the morph and shrinks it after, and `NotchPanel.show` lays the content out at the new size first so the shape stays centred. Content is keyed by `ContentID` and comes and goes with `ContentTransition`: in after `NotchMotion.contentDelay`, out in `contentOut`. What it shows is passed in as values, so content on its way out keeps the track that stopped. As the resting wings appear, the art grows from `NotchMotion.artScale` and `BarsView` raises the bars one after another (`NotchMotion.barDelay`) in Core Animation, then loops them at 20 fps, the scrolling title's rate; they drop as the notch leaves Resting, and their animations go with the view. The timings live in `NotchMotion` (WarbleModel). The expanded card's glow drifts while a song plays: `DriftGlow` holds each of its two pools as a layer twice the card's size, centred on a bottom corner so its edge never shows, and loops its position and scale in Core Animation at 20 fps, each axis on its own 10 to 17 second there-and-back so the pools never fall into step. A pause eases the pools back to the corners, and the glow is only in the view while the card is open, so nothing runs while it's closed. With Reduce Motion the shape changes at once and only the content fades, and the glow stays still.

When the track changes while the card is open, `ExpandedContent` animates the new track in place, which `ContentID` never sees since it keys only on state and playing. The art is keyed by its image, so new art, or art that arrives after the title (nil to image), replaces the old one in a ZStack: the old art fades and shrinks to `NotchMotion.trackArtScale` in `trackArtOut`, and the new art settles in from that scale with a small spring (`trackArtSpringBounce`), on content only, never the shape, and with a few percent of overshoot that the card's padding has room for. Each line of text is keyed by its own string, so a line that changes fades out and the new one slides up `trackLineOffset` points, the title first and the artist and album `trackLineStagger` apart (`NotchMotion.trackLineStart`); a line that stays the same, such as an artist across an album, does not move. The art tile glides to a new width over `trackTileWidth`, so the text slides over rather than jumping. These transitions only run for a change while the card is on screen, so opening it adds nothing to the hover path, and there are no timers. With Reduce Motion the scale and slide are dropped and the art and text only fade.
The playback controls answer a press. `NotchButtonStyle.notchPress(nudge:)` restyles the label from the button's own `isPressed`, so the action still runs on mouse down and up as before and nothing waits on the animation: the label shrinks to `NotchMotion.pressScale` in `pressDown` and springs back on `Native.pressRelease` (under-damped, so it overshoots a little). Previous and next also slide `pressNudge` points toward the skip direction while held, which stays inside the 30-point target and the panel. The play and pause symbols swap with `.contentTransition(.symbolEffect(.replace))` on `Native.symbolSwap`. With Reduce Motion nothing scales or slides, the symbol swaps at once, and a held button only dims to 70%. The volume and favourite buttons and the Settings preview keep `.notch`, the hover wash alone, because the preview is a picture and those icons gain little from a bounce.
Volume and speaker changes ease in the open card. The volume bar's fill eases over `NotchMotion.volumeEase` (`Native.volumeEase`) when the volume changes from the scroll wheel, a key or mute, but `VolumeSlider` turns the animation off while the pointer is dragging the bar (`isDragging`, set in the drag's `onChanged` and cleared in `onEnded`), so the fill stays under the pointer. The speaker symbol and `OutputButton`'s output symbol swap with the `.symbolEffect(.replace)` content transition over `NotchMotion.statusSymbolSwap` (`Native.statusSymbolSwap`), and the volume bar and the "macOS kept the sound" note cross-fade over the same time in `ExpandedContent`. With Reduce Motion the bar and symbols change at once, and the bar and note still fade, since nothing moves.
## Small delights

Content inside the panel has a few springs of its own, and none moves the shape. The heart (`FavouriteButton`) swells to `NotchMotion.heartPopScale` and springs back when a track is liked (`Native.heartPop`, then `heartSettle`), and only settles when it's unliked; the first time it shows beside the title it scales up from `heartAppearScale` and fades in (`Native.heartIn`). Shuffle's and repeat's on-dot scales in from `dotScale` and out again (`Native.dotChange`), and their symbol gives a `symbolEffect(.bounce)` whenever the mode or the symbol changes, including repeat's change to "once". The nothing-playing note and the play-again card rise `riseDistance` points into place as they appear (`Native.rise`), starting `recallDelay` after the shape starts to move; the card's art, text and play icon start `recallStagger` apart, and the play icon grows to `playHoverScale` while the pointer is on the card (`Native.playHover`). All of it runs once on appear, on a click or on hover, with no loop or timer, so an idle empty notch uses no CPU. With Reduce Motion the heart and dots only fade, the symbols don't bounce, and the note and card appear in place with their usual fade. Every overshoot is a few points, inside the panel's frame, which is as large as the largest state.
