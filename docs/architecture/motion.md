# Motion

Read this before changing how the notch moves between states. Back to the [architecture index](../../CLAUDE.md#architecture).

After `NotchSurface.apply(_:)` takes the store's state, `NotchSurface.update` sets `NotchModel.state` and `isPlaying` together inside one `Native.morph`, so every change of state, such as playback stopping under the open notch, morphs the shape and cross-fades the content; it grows the panel before the morph and shrinks it after, and `NotchPanel.show` lays the content out at the new size first so the shape stays centred. Content is keyed by `ContentID` and comes and goes with `ContentTransition`: in after `NotchMotion.contentDelay`, out in `contentOut`. What it shows is passed in as values, so content on its way out keeps the track that stopped. As the resting wings appear, the art grows from `NotchMotion.artScale` and `BarsView` raises the bars one after another (`NotchMotion.barDelay`) in Core Animation, then loops them at 20 fps, the scrolling title's rate; they drop as the notch leaves Resting, and their animations go with the view. The timings live in `NotchMotion` (WarbleModel). The expanded card's glow drifts while a song plays: `DriftGlow` holds each of its two pools as a layer twice the card's size, centred on a bottom corner so its edge never shows, and loops its position and scale in Core Animation at 20 fps, each axis on its own 10 to 17 second there-and-back so the pools never fall into step. A pause eases the pools back to the corners, and the glow is only in the view while the card is open, so nothing runs while it's closed. With Reduce Motion the shape changes at once and only the content fades, and the glow stays still.
